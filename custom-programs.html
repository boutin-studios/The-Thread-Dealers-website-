How ordering works
  1. 1

    Scope it together

    We talk through what it is for, who uses it and the quality you expect.

  2. 2

    Sample and test

    We sample and test every new piece before anything is sold, so the quality holds up.

  3. 3

    Approve and sign

    You approve the sample and details, then sign the order confirmation.

  4. 4

    Produce and deliver

    Production starts once you sign. We keep you updated until it arrives.
